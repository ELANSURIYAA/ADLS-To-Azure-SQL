1. Business Understanding

Business Domain:
Healthcare Member Data Integration

Business Entities:
Member, Enrollment, PCPHistory, CommunicationPreference, MemberAddress, AuditLog

Business Objective of the Pipeline:
To ingest, validate, transform, and route member-related data from the EDH source systems to target Dataverse CRM tables, ensuring strict data quality, format standardization, referential integrity, and auditability for downstream business processes and compliance.

Key Business Transformations:
- Field derivations and format standardization (ISO 8601 dates, E.164 phone, RFC 5322 email, USPS addresses)
- Mandatory field validation and error routing
- Conditional splits for valid/error records
- Default value assignment (SourceSystem)
- Audit log generation
- Referential integrity enforcement (MemberID foreign key)

3. Detailed Functional Test Cases

Test Case ID: FTC_01
Title: Validation of mandatory Member fields and routing of invalid records
Business Scenario: Member records must have FirstName, LastName, and BirthDate populated and non-empty to be considered valid.
Input Condition: SourceMember record with FirstName, LastName, or BirthDate missing or blank.
Expected Behavior: Record is flagged with validation errors and routed to ErrorRecords sink. Only records with all mandatory fields populated proceed to ValidMemberRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_02
Title: Standardization and validation of Member phone numbers (E.164)
Business Scenario: MemberPhone and OtherPhone must be standardized and validated to E.164 format for downstream processing.
Input Condition: SourceMember record with MemberPhone or OtherPhone present but not in E.164 format (e.g., missing +country code, extra characters).
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid E.164 phone numbers are retained and standardized in ValidMemberRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_03
Title: Standardization and validation of Member email (RFC 5322)
Business Scenario: Email addresses must be standardized and validated to RFC 5322 format.
Input Condition: SourceMember record with Email present but not matching RFC 5322 pattern (e.g., missing @, invalid domain).
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid emails are standardized and retained in ValidMemberRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_04
Title: Validation of mandatory Enrollment fields and routing of invalid records
Business Scenario: Enrollment records require PlanName, PlanStatus, and MemberID to be populated and non-empty.
Input Condition: SourceEnrollment record with PlanName, PlanStatus, or MemberID missing or blank.
Expected Behavior: Record is flagged with validation errors and routed to ErrorRecords sink. Only records with all mandatory fields populated proceed to ValidEnrollmentRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_05
Title: Standardization and validation of CareManagerPhone (E.164)
Business Scenario: CareManagerPhone must be standardized and validated to E.164 format.
Input Condition: SourceEnrollment record with CareManagerPhone present but not in E.164 format.
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid E.164 phone numbers are retained and standardized in ValidEnrollmentRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_06
Title: Validation of mandatory PCPHistory fields and phone format
Business Scenario: PCPHistory records require MemberID to be populated and PhoneNumber to be E.164 format if present.
Input Condition: SourcePCPHistory record with MemberID missing or PhoneNumber not in E.164 format.
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid records are retained in ValidPCPHistoryRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_07
Title: Validation of mandatory CommunicationPreference fields
Business Scenario: CommunicationPreference records require MemberID and CommunicationType to be populated and non-empty.
Input Condition: SourceCommunicationPreference record with MemberID or CommunicationType missing or blank.
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid records are retained in ValidCommunicationPreferenceRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_08
Title: Validation of mandatory MemberAddress fields and postal code format
Business Scenario: MemberAddress records require MemberID, Line1, City, State, and PostalCode to be populated and PostalCode to match USPS format.
Input Condition: SourceMemberAddress record with any mandatory field missing or PostalCode not matching USPS 5-digit or 9-digit format.
Expected Behavior: Validation error is generated; record is routed to ErrorRecords. Valid records are retained in ValidMemberAddressRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_09
Title: Routing of valid records to target tables, invalid to error log
Business Scenario: All entities must route valid records to their respective target tables and invalid records to the centralized error log.
Input Condition: Any source record for Member, Enrollment, PCPHistory, CommunicationPreference, or MemberAddress with or without validation errors.
Expected Behavior: Valid records are written to target tables (SinkMember, SinkEnrollment, etc.). Records with validation errors are written to SinkErrorRecords.

Test Case ID: FTC_10
Title: Audit log record generation for valid Member loads
Business Scenario: For every valid Member load, an audit log entry must be generated capturing process, entity, timestamps, and outcome.
Input Condition: Successful processing of ValidMemberRecords.
Expected Behavior: An AuditLogRecords entry is generated for each valid Member record loaded, capturing LogEntryKey, TargetEntity, timestamps, and outcome.

Test Case ID: FTC_11
Title: Referential integrity enforcement between entities (MemberID)
Business Scenario: Enrollment, PCPHistory, CommunicationPreference, and MemberAddress records must reference valid MemberID values present in the Member entity.
Input Condition: Source records with MemberID values not present in Member records.
Expected Behavior: Referential integrity is enforced; records with invalid MemberID are flagged and routed to ErrorRecords. Only records with valid MemberID references are loaded to target tables.
